Hazbin Hotel

Redemption is hard work.

Charlie Morningstar, Princess of Hell, opens a hotel with one impossible goal: rehabilitate sinners so they can be redeemed and enter Heaven — before the yearly Extermination wipes them out. Armed with optimism, her girlfriend Vaggie, and a suspiciously smiling deal-maker called Alastor… what could go wrong?

Helluva Boss

Murder. But make it family business.

Down in Imp City, Blitzø founds I.M.P. — Immediate Murder Professionals — an assassination agency that travels to the living world through a magical grimoire to kill targets on behalf of Hell's denizens. With Moxxie's aim, Millie's rage and Loona's attitude, business is booming.
